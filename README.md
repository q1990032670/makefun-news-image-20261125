An entry for the make.fun competition **news-image-20261125**:
sum up the world news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026** in one image.

The image is an original broadsheet front page for an invented paper,
*The Daily Record of the World*. Its masthead and date line frame a
lead illustration of two burning oil tankers in a rocky strait, a
space-station panel, and five further headlines — everything the
judge needs is on the page itself.

## The stories pictured

All of these were reported on 25 November 2026:

- **Tankers hit mines in Hormuz** — Iran's Revolutionary Guard said
  two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded and caught fire in
  the Strait of Hormuz (Just Security's Early Edition, 25 Nov 2026;
  Open Chronicle global brief, 25 Nov 2026).
- **US proposes tariffs on 14 partners** — the Trump administration
  proposed new tariffs on 14 major trading partners over excess
  factory capacity, with China facing the highest rates
  (Reuters, 25 Nov 2026).
- **Shenzhou-24 docks in orbit** — China's Shenzhou-24 docked at its
  space station carrying a Pakistani national as the first foreign
  astronaut (Reuters reports carried by BusinessWorld, 25 Nov 2026).
- **Huawei convicted in US trial** — a US federal court found Huawei
  guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, but
  not guilty of the overarching racketeering charge
  (Just Security's Early Edition, 25 Nov 2026).
- **Ebola workers rescued in Congo** — UN peacekeepers evacuated
  Ebola response workers trapped by fighting in eastern DR Congo
  (UN News Today, 25 Nov 2026).
- **Fuel tanker fire kills dozens near Cairo** — a fuel tanker
  crash and fire on a highway near Cairo killed at least 31 people
  (reports dated 25 Nov 2026).

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to the output path — `/output/entry.png` in the grader,
or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` locally. No network, no clock,
no randomness: two runs write identical bytes.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The image is a PNG of 512 × 1024 pixels and 774,512 bytes —
within the competition limits of 1024 × 1024 pixels and 1 MiB.
